SCENARIO: You are building a customer support resolution agent using the Claude Agent SDK. The agent handles high-ambiguity requests like returns, billing disputes, and account issues. It has access to your backend systems through custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools (get_customer, lookup_order, process_refund, escalate_to_human). Your target is 80%+ first-contact resolution while knowing when to escalate.

QUESTION: Transcript review reveals two escalation failure modes in the agent. Customers who vent with figurative language such as "this is the last straw" get escalated even though they simply want a quick refund, while a calmly worded message threatening legal action over a disputed charge was handled autonomously without calling escalate_to_human. The current system prompt drives escalation decisions using a list of trigger phrases(트리거 문구 목록을 사용하여). Which prompt revision most improves escalation decisions on customer phrasings the team has never seen before?

**선택지**:
1. Expand the trigger list with every figurative expression and legal phrase found in past transcripts, mapped to escalate or resolve => 단순 하드코딩 패턴이서 오답
2. Add a system prompt instruction telling the agent to weigh the customer's underlying intent rather than their exact wording
3. Run each message through a sentiment score and require escalation whenever negativity exceeds a calibrated threshold
4. Swap the phrase list(문구 목록 교체) for a few **contrasting transcript examples**(대비되는 예시), each explaining why escalation was or was not the right call => 정답

**설명**:
- 팀이 이전에 접해보지 못한 새로운 고객 표현(Unseen phrasings)"이 들어왔을 때, 단순 단어 일치나 겉으로 드러난 감정에 의존하지 않고 에스컬레이션 여부를 정확히 판단하게 만드는 것
- 대비되는 예시와 그 판단의 이유(Chain of Thought)를 명시해 주는 퓨샷 프롬프팅 패턴
- LLM이 문맥과 본질적 위험도를 스스로 "추론"할 수 있는 기준(Heuristic)을 제공

**문제 구분 포인트, 어려운 표현**:
- reveals: 밝혀졌다
- Customers who vent: 화풀이하는 고객
- figurative: 비유적인
- figurative expression: 비유적인 표현
- this is the last straw: 참는 것도 한계다
- legal phrase: 문구
- to weigh: 파악하도록
